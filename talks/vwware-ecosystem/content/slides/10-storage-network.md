## Storage

- Datastores hold VM files
- External arrays (SAN/NAS) or pooled local disks (vSAN)
- VMFS and NFS are common datastore types

---

## Networking

- Virtual switches connect VMs to each other and to physical networks
- VLANs separate traffic
- NSX adds overlays and micro-segmentation
