title: Gotchas to Plan For
slug: gotchas
layout: bullets
eyebrow: Lessons learned
notes: These are common planning issues, not criticisms of any product. Overcommitting resources is the classic mistake. Licensing models differ by product and have changed; always review current terms.
---
- Overcommitted CPU or memory degrades every VM
- Shared storage is a single point of contention
- Snapshots are not backups
- Keep hosts and management software version-compatible
- Review licensing and support terms before scaling
