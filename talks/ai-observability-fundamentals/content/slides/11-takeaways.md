### Measure

Track user-facing latency, errors, and traffic before tuning components.

### Connect

Use trace context and consistent service identity to relate logs, metrics, and traces.

### Protect

Collect the least sensitive, bounded data that can answer the operational question.

### Improve

Use the evidence to make a change, then verify both system behavior and task outcomes.
