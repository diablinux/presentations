# The Production Mindset: A Checklist

Achieving production readiness is not a one-time switch; it's adopting a continuous mindset across the entire lifecycle of your application.

**Are you doing this?**
*   ✅ **Automating:** Using CI/CD to deploy safely and repeatably.
*   ✅ **Monitoring:** Having a full observability stack (Metrics, Logs, Traces) in place.
*   ✅ **Planning:** Having documented DR and maintenance procedures.
*   ✅ **Optimizing:** Right-sizing resources to balance performance with cost.
