# What Does Production Readiness Mean?

It means moving from a successful sandbox environment to a robust, reliable system that meets business continuity requirements.

**Key Pillars:**
*   Reliability (Uptime)
*   Security (Defense in Depth)
*   Observability (Knowing when things break)
*   Maintainability (Easy to upgrade/fix)
