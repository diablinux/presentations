## Lean Kubernetes when

- You want maximum flexibility and portability
- Your team enjoys assembling the stack
- A managed cloud service already fits

---

## Lean OpenShift when

- You want an integrated, supported platform
- Security defaults and compliance matter
- Many teams need a consistent experience
