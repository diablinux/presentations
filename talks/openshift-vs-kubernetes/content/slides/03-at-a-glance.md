## Kubernetes

- Upstream, community-driven project
- Core orchestration you assemble yourself
- Choose your own networking, ingress, registry, monitoring
- Runs almost anywhere; many distributions and managed services

---

## OpenShift

- Red Hat's Kubernetes-based platform (OKD is the community upstream)
- Integrated console, registry, monitoring and CI/CD options
- Opinionated defaults and tested components
- Commercial support through a subscription
