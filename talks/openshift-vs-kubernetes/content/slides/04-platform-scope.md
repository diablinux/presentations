---
### Kubernetes: build your platform

- Pick a CNI, ingress controller, registry, logging and monitoring
- Maximum flexibility
- Your team owns integration and upgrades of each piece

---

### OpenShift: use a platform

- Many of these pieces come preselected and integrated
- Faster path to a working cluster
- Less freedom to swap core components
